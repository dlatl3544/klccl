Principles of this library

What we include, how we translate, and on what terms we publish.

What we include

Public domain only
Only works whose authors died more than 70 years ago, in editions that are themselves in the public domain, are included. Calvin's works are translated from Calvin's collected works (Ioannis Calvini Opera, 1863–1900) and sixteenth-century first editions.
What we do not use
Modern critical editions (Supplementa Calviniana, new collected editions, etc.), modern translations still under copyright, and introductions, covers, or e-book editions added by other sites are not used.

How we translate

Directly from the original
Works are translated directly from the original (French, Latin, etc.), not through an English translation.
Page-by-page parallel text
The original and the translation are aligned page by page in the source edition (e.g. CO 34:n). Click "Cite" next to a page marker to copy a citation.
Style
Sermons are rendered in the formal Korean polite style. Bible quotations follow the Korean Revised Version (1961), whose copyright has expired.
The Scripture box at the start of a sermon
The Korean Revised Version text is set beside a translation of the French Bible text Calvin read when preaching. Since Calvin preaches by working through the words of the text he read, the two are best read together. The translation of Calvin's text is by Claude from the French, and the original is kept below it.
Bible versions included
Besides the Korean Revised Version, the Geneva Bible (1599), the King James Version, the World English Bible, and the Berean Standard Bible are included. All are public-domain texts, and the foreign-language versions are reference material for comparison. The Apocrypha (deuterocanonical books) are not included.
AI draft translation
Generative AI (Claude) was used for the draft translation, and it has not been reviewed by theological experts. Every translation is therefore marked "AI draft translation · not yet theologically reviewed." When quoting, please check the original (CO volume:page).

Terms of use

The translations are released as a CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication. Anyone may copy, distribute, modify, and publish them without permission. Crediting the source is appreciated but not required.

Corrections and records

Error reports are received as described on the Contribute & report errors page. Corrections are recorded with edition numbers so that it is clear which edition was cited.
