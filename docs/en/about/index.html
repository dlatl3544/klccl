About the library

KLCCL (Korean-Language Christian Classics Library) is an online library that translates Western Christian classics into Korean and makes them freely available.

Why we are building it

English readers have long had libraries where the works of the Church Fathers, the Reformers, and the Puritans can be read for free. There has been no such collection in Korean, so pastors, theological students, and church members who do not read the original languages find it hard to reach the primary sources. This library seeks to fill that gap.

What makes it different

FeatureDescription
Christian classics onlyThe library concentrates on patristic, Reformation, and Puritan works.
Direct translationWorks are translated from the original languages, not through an intermediate translation.
Scholarly page citationsThe original and the translation are aligned by the page numbers of the standard edition, so they can be cited directly in papers and sermons.
Open without conditionsTranslations are CC0. Not even a non-commercial condition is attached.

How the library is organized

AuthorsAuthors in chronological order, with their works.
SearchSearch by author, work, title, and Bible reference.

Languages

The library is a Korean library: the translations, the PDFs, and the psalter lyrics are in Korean, alongside the original Latin, French, or Dutch. These English pages translate the menus, descriptions, and guides so that readers who do not read Korean can find their way around and locate the original texts.

How it is run

It is a static site with no sign-up, no advertising, and no donation payments. It runs simply by placing files on free hosting, so there are no operating costs. The manuscripts and site files are kept in a public repository, so anyone can download and keep them or republish them elsewhere.

Affiliation

This library is a private, non-profit project with no affiliation to any denomination, school, or foreign library.
