Klaas Schilder, What Is Hell?

The third edition (1932) by Klaas Schilder (1890–1952), based on his 1919 lecture in Vlaardingen and greatly expanded in 1932. It sets out the biblical teaching on hell from the standpoint of Reformed theology and answers objections.
