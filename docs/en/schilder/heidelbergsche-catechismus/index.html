Klaas Schilder, Commentary on the Heidelberg Catechism

Klaas Schilder's (1890–1952) commentary on the Heidelberg Catechism, begun in 1938 and published in four volumes in 1947–1953. It covers Lord's Days 1–10 (Questions 1–28) and remained unfinished at the author's death.
