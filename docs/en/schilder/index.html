Klaas Schilder 1890–1952

Dutch Reformed theologian. He taught at the Theological Seminary in Kampen and is known for his works on covenant theology and on the sufferings of Christ.

What Is Hell?
The third edition (1932) by Klaas Schilder (1890–1952), based on his 1919 lecture in Vlaardingen and greatly expanded in 1932. It sets out the biblical teaching on hell from the standpoint of Reformed theology and answers objections.		3rd ed. 1932

Commentary on the Heidelberg Catechism
Klaas Schilder's (1890–1952) commentary on the Heidelberg Catechism, begun in 1938 and published in four volumes in 1947–1953. It covers Lord's Days 1–10 (Questions 1–28) and remained unfinished at the author's death.		1947–1953 ed.
