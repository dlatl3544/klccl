Martin Luther 1483–1546

Reformer of Wittenberg. His Ninety-five Theses of 1517 began the indulgence controversy, and he left treatises, sermons, biblical lectures, and letters in Latin and German. His collected works are the Weimar edition (WA, 1883–2009).
