John Calvin, Institutes of the Christian Religion, 1536 and 1539–1554 Editions (CO 1)

Volume 1 of Calvin's collected works (Institutio religionis christianae), in CO column order: the first edition of the Institutes (Basel, 1536: the dedicatory preface to Francis I and six chapters), and the 1539–1554 edition, which combines the 1539 Strasbourg edition with the additions of the 1543, 1545, 1550, 1553, and 1554 editions (the letter to the reader, the dedicatory preface, and 21 chapters).
