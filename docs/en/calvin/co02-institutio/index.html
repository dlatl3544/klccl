John Calvin, Institutes of the Christian Religion, 1559 Edition (CO 2)

Volume 2 of Calvin's collected works (Institutio christianae religionis, 1559). The final Geneva edition of 1559, which Calvin thoroughly revised and rearranged into four books and 80 chapters, in CO column order from the letter to the reader and the dedicatory preface to Francis I through Book 4, Chapter 20, on civil government.
