The current goal: the 59 volumes of Calvin's works

The first goal is to translate all 59 volumes of Calvin's collected works (CO, 1863–1900), published in the nineteenth century, from the original languages into Korean. Since this will take a long time, each part is added as soon as it is finished. After that, the shelves will grow with public-domain works of other Reformers, the Church Fathers, and the Puritans.

Progress

Three commitments of this library

Side by side with the original

Every translation is aligned with the original and its page in the source edition (e.g. CO 34:1), so anyone can check the original text.

Open without conditions

Only public-domain sources are used, and the translations are released under CC0 1.0. Copying, distributing, modifying, and publishing are all free.

A transparent draft translation

The draft translation was made with AI and has not yet been theologically reviewed. Error reports are received and corrected, and the corrections are recorded.
