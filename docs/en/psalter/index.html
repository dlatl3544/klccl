Psalter

The churches of the Reformation put the psalms into meter so that the whole congregation could sing them together. The Genevan Psalter (1562), completed in Calvin's church in Geneva, is the foremost example.

Korean Genevan Psalter, 150 psalms

Click a number to open the score and lyrics. Long psalms with many stanzas are divided into tunes A, B, C … following their sense units. The Korean lyrics are an AI draft adaptation of the psalms of the Korean Revised Version (1961), fitted to the syllable counts of the tunes, and have not yet been theologically reviewed. The scores show no tempo or repeat signs; repeat the tune for as many stanzas as there are lines of lyrics.
